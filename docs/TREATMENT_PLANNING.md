# Treatment Planning: Organic & Chemical Formulation Engine

## 1. Integrated Nutrient Management (INM)
The platform formulates two distinct, complementary treatment paths:

### A. Organic Soil Regeneration Plan
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM)**: 2.5 to 5.0 tonnes/acre basal application. Restores organic carbon and micro-flora.
- **Vermicompost**: 1.0 to 1.5 tonnes/acre for intensive vegetative feeding.
- **Biofertilizers**: Azotobacter (Nitrogen fixing) and PSB (Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria) seed/soil inoculation.
- **Neem Cake**: 100 kg/acre for slow-release nitrogen and nematode pest suppression.
- **Green Manuring**: Dhaincha / Sunhemp in-situ incorporation.

### B. Chemical Fertilizer Plan (STCR Formulation)
- **Area-Proportional Dosing**:
  $$\text{Total Field Requirement (kg)} = \text{Dose per Acre} \times \text{Verified Acreage}$$
- **Standard Bag Units**:
  $$\text{50kg Bags} = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Total Field Requirement}}{50} \right\rceil$$
- **Split Nitrogen Strategy**: Basal (30-40%), Tillering / 1st Top Dressing (30-35%), Panicle / 2nd Top Dressing (30-35%).
- **Micronutrient Correction**: Soil application of Zinc Sulphate ($ZnSO_4 \cdot 7H_2O$) at 10 kg/acre or foliar spray at 0.5% concentration.

### C. Side-by-Side Trade-Off Analysis
| Criterion | Organic Treatment | Chemical Treatment | Recommended INM Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Cost** | Moderate to High (local sourcing dependent) | Lower upfront cost per kg nutrient | Combined basal organic with split chemical |
| **Soil Carbon & Biology** | Greatly improves OC and microbial biomass | Minimal direct contribution to OC | FYM maintains biological buffer against chemical acidification |
| **Speed of Action** | Slow, sustained release over 6-12 months | Rapid uptake within 3-7 days | Chemical satisfies critical early vegetative surges |
| **Environmental Leaching** | Very low leaching risk | High risk of nitrate run-off if misapplied | Split chemical applications prevent groundwater leaching |

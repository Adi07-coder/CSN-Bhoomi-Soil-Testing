## 1. Domain Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|:---|:---|
| **Agro-Climatic Zone** | A land unit in terms of major climate and growing period, which is climatically suitable for a certain range of crops and cultivars. |
| **Available Nitrogen (N)** | The fraction of total soil nitrogen that can be readily absorbed by plant roots, measured in kg/ha via alkaline permanganate distillation. |
| **Available Phosphorus (P)** | The portion of phosphorus accessible for root uptake, typically extracted via Olsen's method (0.5M NaHCO₃) for neutral/alkaline Indian soils. |
| **Available Potassium (K)** | Readily exchangeable and water-soluble potassium available to crop roots, extracted with neutral 1N ammonium acetate. |
| **Basal Application** | Fertilizer broadcast or placed into the soil at or immediately before sowing/planting to support seedling germination. |
| **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)** | Critical developmental stage in Wheat (21–25 days after sowing) where nodal roots initiate, requiring mandatory top-dressed Nitrogen and irrigation. |
| **DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate)** | High-analysis phosphatic fertilizer containing 18% Ammoniacal Nitrogen and 46% Available Phosphorus (P₂O₅). |
| **Electrical Conductivity (EC)** | Measure of the total soluble salt concentration in soil solution, expressed in deciSiemens per meter (dS/m). Values > 2.0 indicate salinity stress. |
| **EPSG:4326** | Standard WGS84 geographic coordinate reference system using longitude and latitude in decimal degrees. |
| **Geodesic Distance / Area** | The shortest distance or true area measured along the curved surface of the WGS84 earth ellipsoid, accounting for planetary oblateness. |
| **ICAR** | Indian Council of Agricultural Research, apex body for coordinating, guiding, and managing research and education in agriculture in India. |
| **Inceptisols** | Young, weakly developed soils with minimal horizon development, widely distributed across the Deccan Plateau and Western Ghats. |
| **MOP (Muriate of Potash)** | Standard potassic fertilizer containing 60% Potassium (K₂O) as Potassium Chloride (KCl). |
| **NDVI** | Normalized Difference Vegetation Index: `(NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)`. Satellite index measuring photosynthetic vigor and green biomass. |
| **Soil Organic Carbon (OC)** | The carbon component of soil organic matter, serving as a primary indicator of biological soil health and nutrient holding capacity. |
| **Soil Health Card** | Standardized printed report issued to farmers recording physical and chemical soil properties alongside crop-specific nutrient recommendations. |
| **Taluka / Tahsil** | Sub-district administrative division in Indian states containing multiple villages and gram panchayats. |
| **Urea** | High-analysis nitrogenous fertilizer containing 46% Amide Nitrogen, widely used as top-dressing. |
| **Vertisols** | Heavy clay soils ("Black Cotton Soils") that shrink and crack during dry seasons and swell when wet, predominant in Maharashtra. |
